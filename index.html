How MenuSnap Works

1

Visit a Restaurant

Check in at restaurants starting with our Tampa launch market, with new cities being added regularly as we expand nationwide.

Take a photo of your menu item and upload it to the app. Each photo earns you XP and helps other foodies discover great dishes.

Gain XP for each photo, with bonus points for trying new cuisines, visiting new restaurants, and maintaining streaks.

Watch your critic profile grow as you explore dining scenes across cities. Unlock achievements and climb leaderboards as MenuSnap expands to new markets.
